Services

Everything below happens in our building, so referrals and paperwork stay to a minimum.

Annual checkups

A yearly visit to catch problems early and keep your records up to date.

  • Physical exam and vital signs
  • Age-appropriate cancer and heart screenings
  • Sports, work and travel physicals

Children's care

Care from the first week home through the teenage years.

  • Well-child visits and developmental checks
  • Childhood and school vaccines
  • School, camp and sports forms

Sick visits

Walk in during open hours or book a same-day time online.

  • Fevers, coughs, ear and throat infections
  • Minor cuts, burns and sprains
  • Rashes and allergic reactions

Ongoing conditions

Regular check-ins and a care plan written with you.

  • Type 1 and type 2 diabetes
  • High blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Asthma and COPD

Women's health

Private, unhurried visits with a clinician you choose.

  • Pap tests and breast exams
  • Contraception counselling
  • Menopause support

Lab tests & vaccines

Blood draws on site, weekdays 8:00 am to 3:00 pm. Most results arrive in 2 days.

  • Blood and urine tests
  • Flu, COVID-19, tetanus and travel vaccines
  • Results shared in your patient portal
