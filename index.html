What we treat

One team handles your everyday health, so you don't have to start over with a new doctor each time.

  • Annual checkups

    Physicals, screenings and a real conversation about how you're doing.

  • Children's care

    Well-child visits, school forms, growth tracking and childhood vaccines.

  • Sick visits

    Colds, infections, rashes, sprains. Walk in or book the same day.

  • Ongoing conditions

    Diabetes, asthma and blood pressure care with regular follow-ups.

  • Women's health

    Pap tests, contraception advice and menopause support.

  • Lab tests

    Blood work done on site, with results in your patient portal.

Your first visit

New patients are welcome. Here's how it works.

  1. Request a time

    Use the online form or call us. We'll confirm within one business day.

  2. Bring your details

    Photo ID, insurance card and a list of any medicines you take.

  3. Meet your doctor

    Plan for about 40 minutes. We'll set up your patient portal before you leave.
